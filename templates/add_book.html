Tips for Listing Your Textbook

  • Include the correct textbook title and author.
  • Provide the ISBN and course information.
  • Be honest about the book's condition.
  • Set a reasonable price.
  • Upload a clear picture of your textbook.
  • Arrange exchanges in safe, public campus locations.
